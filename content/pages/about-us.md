## Our story

Zensys was founded in 2008 and is headquartered in Chennai. It grew into a one-stop provider of CRM, payroll software and SMS services, serving clients in Chennai and across India.

Our speciality is **customised payroll**. Over 200 installations later, Zensys payroll is trusted in particular by the **automobile industry**, where multi-shift attendance, biometric devices and statutory compliance make salary processing demanding.

Our SMS services date back to 2007, helping corporates send transactional alerts, OTPs and campaigns reliably. More recently, our **partnership with Zoho** has added Bigin by Zoho CRM, which we implement and customise for small and growing businesses.

## What we believe

Customer satisfaction matters as much as the quality of our products. That approach has built a loyal and growing customer base over the years, and it shapes how we work:

- **Recommend the right fit.** We study your process before suggesting a solution, not the other way round.
- **Stay after go-live.** Setup, hands-on training and ongoing support are part of every engagement.
- **Make systems work together.** CRM, SMS and payroll set up to share information instead of living in silos.
- **Compliance and security first.** We guide you on keeping operations secure and legally compliant.
