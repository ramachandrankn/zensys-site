Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. zensys.in uses a small number of them.

## Cookies we use

| Purpose | Provided by | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Remembering your cookie choice | zensys.in | Stores that you closed the cookie notice so it isn't shown again |
| Website analytics | Zoho PageSense | Measures visits, clicks and scrolling so we can improve pages |
| Live chat | Zoho SalesIQ | Keeps your chat conversation connected as you move between pages |

Analytics and chat scripts load only after you interact with the page.

## Controlling cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site will still work, although live chat may not.
