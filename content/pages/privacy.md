This policy explains what personal information Zensys ("we", "us") collects through zensys.in and how we use it. If you have questions, write to [info@zensys.in](mailto:info@zensys.in).

## What we collect

- **Details you send us.** When you use the enquiry form, email, call or WhatsApp us, we receive the information you provide, such as your name, company, phone number, email address and requirement.
- **Usage information.** We use Zoho PageSense to understand how visitors use the site (pages visited, clicks, scrolling and device type) so we can improve it.
- **Chat conversations.** If you use the live chat, the conversation is handled by Zoho SalesIQ.

## How we use it

We use your information to respond to your enquiry, arrange demos, provide quotes, deliver and support the products you buy from us, and improve our website. We do not sell your personal information.

## Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run our business, such as Zoho (analytics, chat and CRM) and our hosting provider, and only as needed for those services. We may disclose information if required by law.

## How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and customer information for as long as needed to serve you and to meet legal and accounting obligations.

## Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing [info@zensys.in](mailto:info@zensys.in). You can also control cookies through your browser settings; see our [cookie policy](/cookies-policy/).
