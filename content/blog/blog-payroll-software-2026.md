## What Is Payroll Software?

Payroll software automates the calculation and disbursement of employee salaries, pulling together attendance, leave, deductions, bonuses, and tax rules through one connected platform. Businesses use it to process pay, generate payslips, and produce the reports compliance requires.

Unlike a one-off calculation done manually each month, payroll software applies consistent rules automatically, reducing the chance of the same error repeating cycle after cycle.

## How Does Payroll Software Work?

Businesses set up employee data – salary structure, attendance, leave balances, applicable deductions – once inside the platform. Each pay cycle, the software pulls this data together and calculates net pay automatically, and generated reports track exactly how each figure was reached.

Payroll runs can be structured by department, location, or pay grade, letting businesses apply the right rules to the right group instead of one blanket calculation for everyone.

Pre-configured statutory rules ensure payroll runs stay compliant, reducing the risk of miscalculated deductions or rejected filings.

Detailed reports show salary breakdowns, failed calculations, and payroll trends, helping businesses catch issues before payday.

### Scheduled Payroll Runs

Payroll cycles can be scheduled in advance for month-end, festivals, or bonus payouts, reducing last-minute manual processing.

Payroll platforms can integrate with HR systems and accounting software, letting payroll runs pull directly from existing employee data.
