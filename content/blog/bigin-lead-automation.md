## Introduction

For many small businesses, leads arrive through multiple channels — phone calls, WhatsApp messages, emails, website forms, and referrals. Without a structured system, it becomes difficult to track who to follow up with, when to follow up, and what stage each prospect is at.

Relying on spreadsheets, sticky notes, or memory often leads to missed follow-ups, delayed responses, and lost sales opportunities. As the number of leads grows, manual tracking becomes even harder to manage.

Bigin by Zoho CRM offers a simple, automated way for small businesses to capture leads, organise them into pipelines, and trigger timely follow-ups. It helps sales teams stay on top of every conversation without depending on manual reminders.

## What Is Lead Management Automation in Bigin CRM?

Lead management automation in Bigin by Zoho CRM refers to the system’s ability to automatically capture, organise, and track leads as they move through the sales process. Instead of manually noting down enquiries, the CRM records lead details as soon as they come in.

The platform then applies automated workflows to assign leads to the right team member, schedule follow-up tasks, and send timely reminders. This reduces manual effort while ensuring no lead is overlooked.

## How Does Bigin CRM Automate Follow-Ups?

Bigin captures leads from web forms, email, social channels, and manual entry into a single, centralised workspace. Once a lead is added, automation rules can assign owners, create tasks, and set follow-up reminders based on predefined triggers.

As the lead progresses through pipeline stages, the system can automatically send follow-up emails, notify the assigned team member, and update the next action required. This keeps the sales process moving without relying on manual tracking.
