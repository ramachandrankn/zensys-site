Capture every lead. Follow up on time. Close deals with clarity. [Bigin CRM](/service/bigin-by-zoho-crm/) brings information, pipelines, communication, and sales actions into one workspace.

## Introduction

Sales growth depends on more than finding new leads. Businesses must organise customer information, respond quickly, schedule follow-ups, track every opportunity, and guide each deal through a process until it is won or closed.

When leads are stored in spreadsheets, inboxes, notebooks, and messaging accounts, sales teams lose visibility. Enquiries are forgotten, conversations become difficult to trace, and managers cannot see which deals need attention.

Bigin by Zoho CRM simplifies this work through a pipeline-centric platform for small businesses. It centralises customer records, sales stages, activities, communication, automation, and reporting for consistent selling.

## What Is Bigin CRM?

Bigin by Zoho CRM is a customer relationship management platform designed to help small businesses manage leads, contacts, companies, deals, conversations, and follow-up activities. Its visual pipeline approach makes the sales journey easier to understand and manage.

Instead of operating with disconnected files and applications, sales representatives can work from one organised system. Customer details, deal value, stage, tasks, emails, calls, notes, and interaction history remain connected to the relevant record.

## How Does Bigin CRM Simplify Sales?

Bigin represents sales as customisable stages. Enquiries enter the pipeline, move through qualification and discussion, progress to proposal or negotiation, and reach a won or lost outcome according to the organisation’s workflow.

Users can view the next activity, assign responsibility, update information, communicate with prospects, and monitor progress. Automation reduces repetitive administration, while dashboards help managers identify bottlenecks and guide the team using current sales data.

### Visual Sales Pipeline Management

- Displays active deals by current sales stage
- Highlights opportunities needing immediate attention

### Sales Dashboards and Analytics

- Pipeline value by sales stage

- Custom charts and key performance indicators

### Centralised Customer Records

Bigin keeps contact details, company information, deal history, products, notes, activities, and communications together. This central view gives authorised teams consistent customer context.

### Customisable Sales Stages

Bigin lets businesses customise pipeline stages and fields to match their sales process, from enquiry and qualification through proposal, negotiation, approval, closure, onboarding, or renewal.

### Lead Capture Forms

Custom forms capture enquiries and prospect details directly in Bigin, helping teams respond systematically without copying information between files.

### Multichannel Customer Communication

Email, calls, WhatsApp, and meeting options connect conversations with CRM records, giving teams complete interaction history and context at each sales stage.

### Workflow Automation

Workflows trigger alerts, tasks, emails, and updates when conditions are met. Automation improves consistency and gives representatives more time for valuable customer conversations and deals.

### Sales Activity Management

Activity management plans calls, meetings, tasks, reminders, and deadlines against CRM records. A structured list reduces missed commitments and clarifies the next sales action for users.

### Team Pipelines and Collaboration

Team pipelines, activities, notes, calendar views, and mentions help customer-facing teams coordinate work. Sales, onboarding, operations, and service teams can manage connected processes without losing information during handovers.

### Mobile CRM and Integrations

Mobile apps let representatives access customer information and update activities while travelling or meeting prospects. Integrations connect Bigin with communication, invoicing, meetings, analytics, and other business workflows.

### Data Administration and Security

Roles, profiles, audit logs, transition rules, duplicate management, single sign-on, and multi-factor authentication support controlled access, clean records, and consistent customer data.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

#### What is Bigin by Zoho CRM?

Bigin is a pipeline-centric CRM for small businesses. It brings customer records, deals, communication, activities, automation, and reporting into an organised platform that is simpler than traditional CRM systems.

#### How does Bigin CRM simplify the sales process?

Bigin combines visual pipelines, custom stages, centralised records, reminders, workflows, communication, and dashboards so teams can track opportunities and complete timely sales actions consistently.

#### Can existing spreadsheet data be imported into Bigin?

Yes. Supported spreadsheet data can be imported into Bigin. Careful field mapping, cleaning, and duplicate review before migration help create a reliable customer database.

#### Can Bigin connect email, calls, and WhatsApp conversations?

Bigin supports email, telephony, WhatsApp, and communication options linked to customer records. Availability depends on edition, region, provider, and implementation.

#### Can a business manage more than one pipeline in Bigin?

Yes. Multiple and team pipelines can support products, sales, onboarding, renewals, or customer operations, with stages configured to match each specific business workflow.
