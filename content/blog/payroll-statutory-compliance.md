## Introduction

Statutory compliance is one of the most demanding parts of running payroll in India. Provident Fund (PF), Employee State Insurance (ESI), Professional Tax (PT), Labour Welfare Fund, and TDS all come with their own rules, deadlines, and filing formats that change from state to state.

Handling these manually increases the risk of calculation errors, missed deadlines, and penalties. Even a small mismatch in contribution amounts or a late filing can trigger compliance notices and damage an organisation’s credibility with employees and authorities alike.

Payroll software removes this burden by automating statutory calculations, generating filing-ready reports, and tracking due dates, so HR and finance teams can stay compliant without constantly monitoring changing regulations.

## What Is Payroll Statutory Compliance?

Payroll statutory compliance refers to the legal requirement for businesses to calculate, deduct, and remit government-mandated contributions and taxes from employee salaries, and to file the related returns within prescribed timelines.

This includes PF and ESI contributions, Professional Tax, Labour Welfare Fund, and income tax deductions under TDS. Non-compliance can lead to financial penalties, interest charges, and legal complications for the employer.

## How Does Payroll Software Simplify Compliance?

Payroll software applies the latest statutory rates and rules automatically during salary processing, calculating PF, ESI, PT, and TDS deductions without manual intervention. This removes the guesswork involved in tracking rate changes across different states and wage slabs.

The system generates challans and return-ready reports in the required formats, and sends reminders ahead of filing deadlines. This ensures that statutory dues are calculated correctly and submitted on time, every pay cycle.
