## Who it's for

Cloud payroll suits growing companies, startups and businesses with branches, field staff or remote HR teams. It removes the need for an in-house server and gives managers access from wherever they are.

## How we implement it

1. **Requirement discussion** about your pay structure, locations and attendance methods.
2. **Live demo** on a setup close to your own.
3. **Setup and customisation** of salary heads, leave policies and approvals.
4. **Data migration** of employee records and balances.
5. **User training** for HR, managers and employees on the mobile app.
6. **Go live**, with ongoing support from our team.
